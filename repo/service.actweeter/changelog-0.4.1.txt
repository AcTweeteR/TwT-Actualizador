AcTweeteR Service 0.4.1

- Wait for Kodi's actual PVR initialization state after requesting XStream's
  native PVR synchronization; report timeout, cancellation, or API-stage errors
  without repeating the sync request or modifying IPTV Simple settings.
- After explicit user consent, enable and read back XStream's version-pinned
  auto_sync_pvr setting so the profile refresh route invokes PVR sync.
- Treat PVR as ready only after Kodi reports an available, non-scanning client
  and at least one real TV channel group with channels.
- Keep EPG observation separate from installation completion; no guide entries
  are reported as observed unless Kodi returns a real broadcast.
