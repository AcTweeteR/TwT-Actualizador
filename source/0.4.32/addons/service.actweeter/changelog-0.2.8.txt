AcTweeteR Service 0.2.8 — completion gates for required setup

- Treat IPTV/XStream, PVR, trailer provider and YouTube as required setup
  components in the service-status UI.
- Persist only allowlisted setup check statuses; never persist credential data.
- Do not mark setup complete merely because XStream settings fields exist.
- Require runtime evidence for provider authentication, catalogs, live TV/EPG,
  trailer search/resolution/playback and skin integration before completion.
- Keep incomplete setup resumable and preserve legacy step labels on upgrade.
- Clarify that YouTube may require user-owned API/OAuth configuration.
- Remove automatic Master-profile credential and structural-settings reuse from
  the service runtime; users configure their own profile through Kodi/add-ons.
