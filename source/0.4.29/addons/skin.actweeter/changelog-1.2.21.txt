AcTweeteR Skin 1.2.21

- Spotlight movie and series selection now opens the shared Kodi information
  sheet; playback stays on the explicit Play action.
- Home, Spotlight, and library detail views no longer start trailer playback
  from focus/hover events. Automatic-preview controls are hidden because Kodi
  does not distinguish mouse hover from remote/touch focus; manual trailer
  actions remain available.
- Kodi-native mouse focus and touch gesture handling are unchanged. Android
  touch and trailer runtime validation remain pending.
